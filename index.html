About

Two disciplines, one seamless practice

I am an AI Systems Engineer and problem solver specialized in optimizing the hidden layers where machine intelligence meets hardware architecture. My expertise spans the entire computational stack, allowing me to bridge the traditional divide between operating system kernels and deep learning models.Rather than working within isolated silos, I focus on cross layer engineering to resolve the modern AI efficiency crisis. My core specialties include: Kernel-Level Workload Optimization: Introspecting and manipulating OS schedulers, managing hardware CPU affinity, and tuning process priorities to maximize hardware efficiency for AI workloads. Production-Grade Vision Infrastructure: Architecting end-to-end computer vision and sequence pipelines built for real-world accuracy and optimal deployment. High-Performance Parallel Computing: Designing decoupled master-worker distributed data pipelines and DAG-based execution models to break through traditional data bottlenecks. Advanced AI Architectures & Integration: Engineering state-of-the-art retrieval systems, GraphRAG knowledge frameworks, autonomous agentic operations, and custom Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers. I thrive on complex, systemic bottlenecks that refuse to stay inside a single layer of the stack.
